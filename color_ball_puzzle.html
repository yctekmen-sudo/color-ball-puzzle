How to play

  • Each square shows a color with exactly 1 hidden ball per color.
  • 1 ball per row/column; no balls in adjacent squares.
  • Single-Click / Drag: Mark squares with ✕.
  • Double-Click: Reveal a square — wrong guesses lose 1 life!
  • Metrics Card: Click anytime to recalculate remaining difficulty and check board uniqueness!
